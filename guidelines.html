KollabiX

Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 2026

Our Goal

KollabiX exists to connect local Puerto Rico businesses with creators for genuine, mutually beneficial collaborations. These Guidelines help keep the community trustworthy, respectful, and safe for everyone.

Be Honest

Represent yourself and your audience accurately. Do not fabricate or exaggerate follower counts, engagement rates, or audience demographics. Do not use fake accounts, bots, or misleading photos.

Be Respectful

Treat every user with respect. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, threats, or abusive language of any kind toward another user will not be tolerated and may result in immediate account suspension.

Honor Your Commitments

If you agree to a Deal, follow through on what was agreed: deliverables, deadlines, and compensation. Repeatedly failing to honor agreed collaborations damages trust in the community and may result in account review or removal.

Keep It Relevant

Use messaging only to discuss legitimate collaborations. Do not use the App to spam, solicit unrelated services, sell unrelated products, or send unsolicited promotional content.

Respect Privacy

Do not share another user's private information (phone number, address, financial details) outside the App without their consent, and do not use information obtained through KollabiX for purposes unrelated to a collaboration.

No Illegal or Harmful Content

Do not post or share content that is illegal, sexually explicit, violent, hateful, or that infringes on someone else's intellectual property rights.

Disclose Sponsored Content

Creators must clearly disclose sponsored or paid content created through a KollabiX Deal (for example with #ad or the platform's paid partnership label), as required by applicable advertising laws and platform rules.

Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates these Guidelines, please report it through the App or email altcreativepr@gmail.com. We are notified of every report, review it, and take action, which may include removing content, warnings, suspension, or permanent account removal. KollabiX also filters objectionable language automatically.

Enforcement

KollabiX reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to remove content, suspend accounts, or permanently ban users who violate these Guidelines, our Terms of Service, or applicable law.
