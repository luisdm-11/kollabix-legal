KollabiX Community Guidelines Last updated: September 2026 Our Goal KollabiX exists to connect local Puerto Rico businesses with creators for genuine, mutually beneficial collaborations. These Guidelines help keep the community trustworthy, respectful, and safe for everyone. Be Honest Represent yourself and your audience accurately. Do not fabricate or exaggerate follower counts, engagement rates, or audience demographics. Do not use fake accounts, bots, or misleading photos. Be Respectful Treat every user with respect. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, threats, or abusive language of any kind toward another user will not be tolerated and may result in immediate account suspension. Zero Tolerance KollabiX has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Content that is abusive, hateful, threatening, harassing, or sexually explicit is removed, and the users who post it are suspended or permanently removed. You can block any user at any time from their profile or your chat with them, and report them so we can act within 24 hours. Honor Your Commitments If you agree to a Deal, follow through on what was agreed: deliverables, deadlines, and compensation. Repeatedly failing to honor agreed collaborations damages trust in the community and may result in account review or removal. Keep It Relevant Use messaging only to discuss legitimate collaborations. Do not use the App to spam, solicit unrelated services, sell unrelated products, or send unsolicited promotional content. Respect Privacy Do not share another user's private information (phone number, address, financial details) outside the App without their consent, and do not use information obtained through KollabiX for purposes unrelated to a collaboration. No Illegal or Harmful Content Do not post or share content that is illegal, sexually explicit, violent, hateful, or that infringes on someone else's intellectual property rights. Disclose Sponsored Content Creators must clearly disclose sponsored or paid content created through a KollabiX Deal (for example with #ad or the platform's paid partnership label), as required by applicable advertising laws and platform rules. Reporting If you experience or witness behavior that violates these Guidelines, please report it through the App (the report option on chats, profiles and deals) or email altcreativepr@gmail.com. We are notified of every report and review it within 24 hours. When content or behavior violates these Guidelines, we remove the content and may warn, suspend, or permanently remove the account responsible. KollabiX also filters objectionable language automatically. Enforcement KollabiX reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to remove content, suspend accounts, or permanently ban users who violate these Guidelines, our Terms of Service, or applicable law.
