Delete your KollabiX account

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app
Open KollabiX, go to Settings > Delete account, and confirm with your password. Deletion is immediate.
Without the app
Email altcreativepr@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my KollabiX account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your profile and photos, deals, applications, connections, conversations and messages, notifications and subscription record.

What we may keep

Payment records kept by Apple, Google or Stripe for legal and tax purposes, and the email used for the free trial (only to prevent repeated free trials). Reports about abuse may be kept for safety reasons.

Canceling an App Store or Google Play subscription is done in your device's subscription settings. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription automatically.
