Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how KollabiX ('we,' 'us,' 'our') collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App. By using KollabiX, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Account information: name, email address, and password (handled securely by our authentication provider). Profile information: photos, business or creator details, categories, location, audience demographics, and social media handles you choose to provide. Usage data: deals posted or applied to, connections, messages you send within the App, and general app activity. Payment information: Creator subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store), Google (Google Play) or Stripe (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay). We receive the status of your subscription, not your full card number. Reports: if you report another user, we receive the reason and details you provide.

How We Use Information

We use your information to: operate and improve the App; match Businesses and Creators; enable messaging and in-app notifications between connected users; process and verify subscription payments; filter objectionable language automatically; review reports and enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines; and communicate with you about your account and support requests.

How Information Is Shared

Your profile information (such as your name, photos, and public stats) is visible to other users of the App as part of the App's core functionality. We share data with service providers who help operate the App, solely to provide the service: Supabase (hosting, database and authentication), Stripe (card payments), Apple and Google (App Store and Google Play purchases), RevenueCat (store subscription status) and Resend (internal email alerts about user reports). We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising or tracking.

Messages

Messages you send within the App are stored so that conversations can be displayed to both participants. Only the participants of a conversation can view its messages in the App. If a conversation is reported, KollabiX may review it to investigate the report and enforce our Terms. Messages that contain language not allowed on KollabiX are rejected automatically.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, deals, connections, conversations, messages, and notifications tied to your account are permanently deleted from our systems. This action is irreversible.

Your Rights

You may access and update most of your profile information directly within the App's Settings. You can delete your account and associated data at any time from Settings > Delete account, or by emailing altcreativepr@gmail.com from the email address on your account. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights regarding your personal data; contact us to make such a request.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted storage and access controls, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

KollabiX is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we learn that we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated 'Last updated' date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to altcreativepr@gmail.com or through Settings > Contact support in the App. KollabiX is operated by Alt Agency (Luis Maldonado), Puerto Rico.
